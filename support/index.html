ONEKEPT

Support

OneKept is a quiet space for one daily priority. Find quick help below.

Restore OneKept Pro

Open OneKept Settings and choose “Restore purchases.” Make sure you are signed in to the Apple Account used for the subscription.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You can also use Apple’s subscription page.

Focus reminders

Reminders are optional and scheduled on your device for the finish time you choose. Enable them in OneKept Settings. If you previously declined permission, enable notifications for OneKept in iOS Settings.

Delete focus history

In OneKept Settings, choose “Delete my data.” This removes focus content stored on that device. The free-use allowance does not reset.
