ONEKEPT

Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

OneKept is designed to work without creating a personal account. Your focus text, completion notes, deadlines, and history stay on your device.

Information we process

We create an anonymous technical identifier through Supabase. To enforce the two free focus limit, Apple DeviceCheck provides a device token and two anti-abuse status bits. We store the anonymous identifier, request identifiers, grant results, and timestamps. We do not receive your Apple ID or a hardware serial number.

Apple processes App Store purchases. RevenueCat processes subscription status and a pseudonymous app user identifier so OneKept can unlock Pro and restore purchases. Local notifications are scheduled on your device.

How we use information

We use this limited data to provide the app, restore access, prevent repeated free-use abuse, diagnose failures, and protect the service. We do not sell personal information, run third-party advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Storage and service providers

Supabase hosts anonymous access and grant records. Apple provides DeviceCheck, App Store billing, and notifications. RevenueCat manages subscription entitlement status. These providers process data under their own security and privacy terms.

Retention and choices

Deleting OneKept removes local focus content. Anti-abuse status may remain associated with the device through Apple DeviceCheck, and limited server records may be retained for service integrity. You can disable notifications in iOS Settings and manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the developer support contact shown on OneKept's App Store page.
